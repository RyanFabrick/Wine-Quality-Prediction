Codebook

Variable Name Role Data Type Description & Notes
fixed_acidity Feature Continuous Based on physicochemical tests. Represents most acids involved with wine.
volatile_acidity Feature Continuous The amount of acetic acid in wine. Very high of levels can lead to a commonly unpleasant, vinegar taste.
citric_acid Feature Continuous Found in small quantities. Citric acid can add 'freshness' and flavor to wines.
residual_sugar Feature Continuous The amount of sugar remaining after fermentation stops.
chlorides Feature Continuous The amount of salt in the wine.
free_sulfur_dioxide Feature Continuous The free form of SO2 exists in equilibrium between molecular SO2 (as a dissolved gas) and bisulfite ion.
total_sulfur_dioxide Feature Continuous Amount of free and bound forms of S02.
density Feature Continuous The density of water is close to that of water depending on the percent alcohol and sugar content.
pH Feature Continuous Describes how acidic or basic a wine is on a scale from 0 (very acidic) to 14 (very basic).
sulphates Feature Continuous A wine additive which can contribute to sulfur dioxide gas (S02) levels.
alcohol Feature Continuous The percent alcohol content of the wine.
quality Target Integer Output variable based on sensory data. A discrete score given by human experts between 0 (very bad) and 10 (excellent).
color / type Categorical String Identifies the variant of the wine (Red or White).
